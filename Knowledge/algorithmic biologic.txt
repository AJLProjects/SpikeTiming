-  Fast-Spiking (FS) Interneurons.


- inhibitory neurons (interneurons) themselves are a minority. In the cortex, roughly 80-85% of neurons are excitatory (mostly pyramidal cells), and 15-20% are inhibitory (GABAergic interneurons).

- FS Neurons within the Inhibitory Population: Fast-Spiking (FS) neurons are a major subtype of inhibitory interneurons. The most common type of FS neuron expresses the protein Parvalbumin (PV+). These PV+ FS neurons make up a significant fraction of all inhibitory interneurons – often estimated at around 40-50% of the GABAergic population in many cortical areas.

- Essentially zero percent (0%) of the excitatory neuron population are considered Fast-Spiking (FS)
